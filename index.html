Interpreting BMI

  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5–24.9
  • Overweight: 25–29.9
  • Obesity: ≥ 30

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. For personalized medical advice, consult a healthcare professional.
